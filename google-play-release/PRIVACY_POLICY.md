# Privacy Policy for Crew Story

**Effective Date**: September 5, 2026  
**Last Updated**: September 5, 2026  

Crew Story ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a privacy-focused, multimodal AI storytelling and picture book creation application. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users, especially families and children.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **Local Data Storage**: All story scripts, chapter contents, customized narrations, and selected illustrations created or modified within the App are stored exclusively on your local device. We do not transmit, sync, or store your story content on any external servers operated by us.
- **API Key (Bring Your Own Key - BYOK)**: When you provide a Google Gemini API Key, it is encrypted and saved strictly on your local device storage. The key is used solely to authenticate direct HTTPS/WSS requests between your device and Google Gemini API services.
- **Audio & Microphone**: The App requests microphone access only when you actively engage in two-way interactive voice storytelling. Audio data is streamed directly to the AI service for real-time speech processing and is not recorded, stored, or analyzed by our application.
- **Images & Photos**: Photo and media permissions are used exclusively to allow you to pick illustrations or photos from your local gallery to attach to your picture books.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services
The App interfaces directly with the following third-party service:
- **Google Gemini API / Google AI Studio**: Used for text generation, speech synthesis, and image generation. Data sent to Google is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google AI Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/terms).

---

### 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Crew Story is suitable for all ages and family reading environments. We do not collect, store, or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 4. Security
All network communications with AI services occur over industry-standard encrypted channels (HTTPS / WSS with TLS encryption).

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
